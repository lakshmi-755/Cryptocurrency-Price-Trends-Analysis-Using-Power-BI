
✅ 📌 Project Description: Cryptocurrency Price Trends Analysis Using Power BI

The Cryptocurrency Price Trends Analysis Dashboard is an interactive data visualization project developed to analyze and understand the price movements of various cryptocurrencies over multiple years. The purpose of this project is to convert raw market data into meaningful insights that help users study trends, volatility, and overall market performance.

This dashboard focuses on major cryptocurrencies such as Bitcoin, Ethereum, Binance Coin, Dogecoin, XRP, Stellar, and NEM, and provides an easy interface to compare their market behavior. Users can filter the data by year (2017–2021) and by cryptocurrency, allowing a dynamic and customized analysis experience.

The project uses Power BI to visualize key financial metrics like Open Price, Close Price, High Price, Low Price, Market Capitalization, and Trading Volume. These are represented using interactive line charts, slicers, and KPI cards. The dashboard highlights daily price fluctuations, overall trends, and major market movements for each selected cryptocurrency.

✅ What is Cryptocurrency?

Cryptocurrency is a digital or virtual form of money that uses blockchain technology to secure transactions.
Unlike regular money issued by a government (like INR or USD), cryptocurrencies are:

Decentralized

Stored online

Recorded on a blockchain

Not controlled by banks or authorities

Examples: Bitcoin, Ethereum, Binance Coin, Dogecoin, XRP, etc.

Cryptocurrency can be used for trading, investment, payments, and smart contracts.

✅ Important Cryptocurrency Terms (Explained Simply)

Below are the terms you need to explain when talking about crypto:

1. Blockchain

A digital public ledger that records all cryptocurrency transactions.
Every transaction is stored in blocks, and all blocks are linked.

2. Coin vs Token

Coin → Native cryptocurrency of a blockchain (e.g., Bitcoin, Ethereum).

Token → Built on another blockchain (e.g., USDT on Ethereum).

3. Mining

The process of validating transactions and creating new coins using computational power.

4. Wallet

A digital wallet used to store cryptocurrencies.

Types:

Hot Wallet – online

Cold Wallet – offline (safer)

5. Exchange

A platform to buy or sell cryptocurrencies
(e.g., Binance, Coinbase, WazirX)

6. Market Cap (Market Capitalization)

The total value of a cryptocurrency.

📌 Formula:
Market Cap = Price × Total Number of Coins in Circulation

7. Volume

The total number of coins traded within a time period (24 hours or more).
High volume = high investor activity.

8. Open Price

Price at which a cryptocurrency starts trading on a given day.

9. Close Price

Price at which it ends trading on a given day.

10. High Price

The maximum price reached during a day.

11. Low Price

The minimum price reached during a day.

12. Volatility

Cryptos change price rapidly → this is called volatility.

13. Bull Market

When prices are continuously rising.

14. Bear Market

When prices are falling or staying low.

15. Decentralization

No single authority controls the currency—power is distributed.

16. Smart Contracts

Programs stored on blockchain that run automatically when conditions are met (used in Ethereum).

If you want, I can also explain advanced terms like halving, staking, NFTs, gas fees, hash rate, nodes, etc.
